FLIGHT RECORDER FOR AI AGENTS

Your AI agent failed. Find exactly where — and fix it without starting over.

Black Box records every model decision and tool call as an OpenTelemetry trace. When a run fails, it points to the step that caused it, explains why with evidence, and replays from that step, re-running only what depends on it, to prove the fix works.
